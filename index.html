About us

We are a Roblox law firm operating in the France roleplay nation. Our staff represent clients, draft and review documents, and advise on how the rules of the nation apply to their situation.

Everything starts in our Discord server, where you can ask for help, follow announcements and meet the team.

How we can help

  • Representation in disputes and hearings
  • Drafting and reviewing contracts
  • Advice for businesses and organisations
  • Guidance on the nation's laws
